Paper Equation (1) – Time-Dependent Edge Traversal Weight:
w_e(t) = [ d_e / v_{e,free} ] · Ĉ_e(t)

Where d_e is segment distance (m), v_{e,free} is free-flow velocity (km/h), and Ĉ_e(t) = max(1.0, v_{e,free} / v̂_e(t)) is the ML-predicted dynamic congestion multiplier evaluated at anticipated arrival time t.
